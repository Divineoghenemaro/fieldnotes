About Fieldnotes

Fieldnotes is a small, ongoing collection of short essays about paying closer attention — to books worth rereading, to the unglamorous middle of making something, to the ordinary corners of a day that usually pass without comment.

There's no schedule. An entry gets written when there's something worth writing down, which is really the whole idea: noticing is the practice, and the writing is just the record of it.

Every entry on this site is stored in the blog database and can be managed from the admin pages. Write freely, edit anything, and delete without ceremony.
